## Optional planning-adapter trial

Before running: fix the scope, starting revision, completion stage, model/effort, baseline condition and comparison method. Separate adapter value (workflow with/without adapter) from overall workflow value (equivalent AI assistance without workflow, retaining the same quality/approval gates). Record setup separately.

Observe fresh Codex and Claude discovery, four reserved unknowns, native Draft grouping, manual projection tampering, interruption/recovery and authority-profile drift. Record outcomes, not just passing content assertions.

Measure wall-clock critical path, active work, tool/CI waits, owner attention/approvals, non-cached input, cached input, output and reasoning subset when available. Deduplicate response IDs across continuations; unknown usage stays unknown. Track rework and defects by discovery layer, acceptance failures, owner-performed technical work and a 14-day defect follow-up. Match scope/risk/completion stage; do not claim superiority from one incomparable trial or from moving work to the owner.

## Delivery-efficiency comparison

Use matched tasks or repeated disposable fixtures with the same scope, starting failures, completion stage, model/effort and quality/approval requirements. Pre-register the delivery unit and comparison: current workflow, optional batch/routine mode, or equivalent AI assistance without the workflow. Keep setup separate. Use at least three matched pairs and alternate their order; treat a small sample as exploratory.

Record per accepted outcome: critical-path elapsed time, active agent time, tool/CI waits, owner-blocked intervals, actual owner attention and prompts, approvals/merges, CI runs and runner-minutes, unique-response usage by agent/phase, rework and defect discovery layer. Keep cached input distinct from unique/non-cached input and reasoning as a subset of output. Sum each response once across continuation files; reconcile cumulative counters, never add them. External-agent usage is included or explicitly unknown. No account-limit percentage or dollar conversion without account-specific evidence.

Keep review depth and tests constant. Seed a stale review, a silenced failure and a weakened assertion in disposable fixtures; require detection, and require consequential changes to stay on the owner route. Include acceptance failures, owner-performed technical work, rollback difficulty and a 14-day defect follow-up. A target (not a prediction): fewer owner actions and at least 20% less elapsed time or non-cached/output usage, with no increase in escaped defects, rework or owner testing burden. If speed comes from lowering acceptance quality or moving work to the owner, the variant fails. Publish raw measurement references and uncertainty on the pilot PR/issue, not in this template.
